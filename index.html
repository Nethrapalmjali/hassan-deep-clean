FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Hassan city and nearby towns including Arsikere, Channarayapatna, Tiptur and Kadur. If you are close to these areas, message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

How do I book a service?

Use the booking form above, tap any WhatsApp button, or call us on 063602 92394 / 88616 49516. Share what needs cleaning and we'll send you a quote and confirm a slot.

Do you bring your own equipment and cleaning materials?

Yes. Our team comes with professional machines (scrubbers, vacuum cleaners), tools and cleaning solutions suited to each surface — you don't need to arrange anything.

How much does deep cleaning cost?

The price depends on the size of the property and the services you choose. Send us your requirement (and photos if possible) on WhatsApp and we'll give you a clear, affordable quote before we start.

Do you clean offices and newly built houses?

Yes — we clean offices, shops and commercial spaces, and we do post-construction cleaning to remove cement, paint and dust so a new house is ready to move into.

Can you help with home shifting too?

Yes. We offer home shifting and relocation with careful packing, loading and transportation of household items. Many customers combine it with a move-in deep clean of the new home.
